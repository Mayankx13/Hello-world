№ 06 / 12 The Concept
What we're building

An understudy that shows up as you — every day, in motion.

A clone of your voice and your face, trained on a few hours of you talking. Each week, it watches what's trending on Instagram and produces three Reels in your style — your hook, your delivery, your face on camera. You wake up, approve, post.

Sample output. This is the rough shape of what the clone produces — three Reels a week, in your voice, on your face, riding what's working now.

i.

Trained on you, not on the internet

A few hours of your calls and a handful of selfie clips. Enough that it sounds like you and shows up as you — not a generic AI version.

ii.

Rides what's working this week, not last year

A trend engine watches Instagram daily. The clone makes Reels in formats that are converting right now, not last year's playbook.

iii.

You approve, you post

Every Reel lands in your inbox as a draft. One tap to publish, or send back for a re-cut. It learns from both.
