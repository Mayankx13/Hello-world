How it works

From walk-in to closed bill in four steps

A deterministic engine does the ranking — fast, explainable, and the same every time. The optional AI layer only writes the friendly explanation.

1

Ask

Five quick questions per category. Admins edit the questions live from the dashboard — no app update.

2

Match

Fit gates first (size, form, features), then a commercial ranking blends margin, ageing and availability.

3

Present

Good · Better · Best, plus one worth-the-stretch — with honest pros & cons and no-cost-EMI lines.

4

Grow

Add the attach basket, log the outcome. It flows into the leaderboard and the command centre.

The platform

One assistant. A whole retail brain behind it.

Built for multi-store electronics retailers — fed live from BUSY ERP or your cloud database.

Guided Sales Assistant

Bilingual, tablet-first, kiosk-ready. Honest Good–Better–Best from live stock in under two minutes.

Gamified leaderboards

Weekly and monthly points within a store and across stores — healthy competition that lifts items-per-bill.

Command Centre

North-star items-per-bill, store comparison, stock-ageing health and category-coverage gaps at a glance.
