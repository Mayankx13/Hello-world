### Why this About works (so you can defend it on a call)

- **First line is the hook, not a greeting.** LinkedIn truncates after ~210 chars on mobile. The first sentence carries the band signal (5 yrs, PwC + Accenture, $-impact, "GenAI on top of that data layer"). Everything else is below the fold.
- **"Messy middle" framing** stakes out territory that distinguishes you from (a) pure DS candidates with no enterprise scars and (b) pure BI devs with no AI. That's *exactly* the lane Tiger / Fractal / Tredence are hiring against.
- **Stack name-drops are in the About, not just headline** — recruiter search re-ranks profiles where the keyword appears in body text.
- **"Open to" line is explicit.** LinkedIn's `#OpenToWork` green-banner is optional; the explicit line ranks you in recruiter search filters either way and looks less desperate than the banner for senior bands.
- **CTA is a conversation, not an application.** Matches your outreach template (#4) and warms inbound from recruiters who don't want to feel like they're cold-pitching.
