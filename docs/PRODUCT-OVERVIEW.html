LIQO

In‑store recommendation & retail‑intelligence platform for multi‑brand electronics stores — turning every salesperson into an expert and every store into a data‑driven operation.
Product: LIQOCompany: Amaflip Category: Retail SaaS (PWA)Markets: North India · 7 stores
Executive summary. LIQO is a mobile‑first web app used on the shop floor of Amaflip's electronics stores. It guides a salesperson through a short conversation with the customer and instantly recommends the best in‑stock air conditioners, TVs, refrigerators and washing machines for that customer's needs and budget — each with a warm, honest, bilingual reason to buy. Behind the counter it doubles as a management platform: live inventory health, demand signals, staff performance, incentives, offers and a full audit trail. Recommendations are produced by a deterministic engine (fast, auditable, always in‑stock), with AI used only to phrase the pitch — so the store can trust every suggestion.

1The problem we solve

Electronics retail is high‑consideration and high‑SKU. A customer picking an AC or fridge faces dozens of models and jargon (tonnage, star rating, inverter, capacity); a salesperson can't hold 2,000 live SKUs, prices and stock in their head, and often defaults to whatever they know — missing the best fit, the upsell, and the attach‑on. Meanwhile management lacks a real‑time view of what's selling, what's ageing, and how each store and salesperson is performing.

LIQO closes both gaps at once: a guided, honest recommendation at the point of sale, and the operational intelligence that falls out of every interaction.

6The recommendation approach — our differentiator

LIQO splits the job in two so recommendations are both trustworthy and persuasive:

Deterministic engine — decides whatAI (Haiku) — decides how to say it
RoleRanks the productsWrites the one‑line pitch
GuaranteeOnly real, in‑stock, in‑budget items; same input → same result; fully explainable & testableWarm, bilingual, human — but constrained to the engine's facts; can't invent or reorder
WhyA store must trust every suggestion and never pitch something it can't sellTemplates feel robotic; language is where AI genuinely helps

The engine is business‑controllable — management can push margin, clear ageing stock, apply offer boosts or exclude brands — all as live, auditable settings.

7Data foundation (DBMS)

A normalised, integrity‑enforced cloud database is the backbone — six domains, one source of truth:

  • Reference — stores, engine configuration
  • Inventory — the hourly live‑stock snapshot from BUSY
  • People — employees, attendance, leaves, milestones, incentives, feedback
  • Customers — profiles, brand affinities, interaction timeline (consent‑gated)
  • Commerce — sales journeys, products suggested, offers, unmet‑demand requests
  • Analytics & Audit — store/demand/performance views + the activity log

8Architecture & technology

Front end

React + TypeScript PWA (installable, offline, mobile‑first, bilingual).

Edge back end

Two Cloudflare Workers — the API and the hourly inventory sync — running close to each store.

Database

Cloudflare D1 (SQLite) — the app reads only this snapshot, never the ERP live.

AI

Claude Haiku 4.5 for the rationale line, tightly constrained with a deterministic fallback.

ERP integration

BUSY (on‑prem MS SQL Server) → a secure, outbound‑only connector pushes stock to the cloud.

Delivery

Cloudflare Pages + GitHub Actions CI/CD — tests‑gated, one‑click, no‑server deploys.

BUSY ERP→Sync connector→Cloudflare D1→ API + engine→PWA   Haiku · rationale

9Security, privacy & compliance

  • Role‑based access — admin / manager / salesperson gates on every screen and API.
  • Secure auth — signed session tokens, hashed passwords, self‑service password change.
  • DPDP‑ready — customer PII is captured only with consent and can be erased on request.
  • Full audit trail — every state‑changing action is logged.
  • Least privilege — the ERP connector uses a read‑only login and sends stock only (no customer data).

10Deployment & scale

Runs on Cloudflare's global edge with separate development and production environments (amaflip.in). It's serverless and stateless at the edge, so adding stores or traffic needs no new infrastructure — the same stack serves 7 stores or 70. Inventory stays fresh automatically; deploys are test‑gated and reversible.
