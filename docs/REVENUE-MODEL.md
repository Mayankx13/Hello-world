# 💰 Revenue Model — Deal Engine

## Primary Revenue: Commission Per Deal

### How It Works
1. System matches buyer + seller
2. You connect them (auto-message via WhatsApp)
3. You facilitate the deal (site visits, negotiation, paperwork)
4. You earn commission on successful closure

### Commission Rates (Chandigarh Tricity standard)

| Property Value | Your Commission | Typical Earning |
|---------------|----------------|-----------------|
| < ₹50 Lakh | 1% from each side | ₹50K - 1L per deal |
| ₹50L - 1 Cr | 1% from each side | ₹1L - 2L per deal |
| ₹1 Cr - 3 Cr | 0.5-1% from each side | ₹1L - 6L per deal |
| > ₹3 Cr | 0.5% from each side | ₹3L+ per deal |

**Typical**: If you close 2-3 deals per month at ₹50L-1Cr range → **₹2-6 Lakh/month**

### Revenue Split with Brokers

When a deal involves a broker from your network:
- **You sourced the buyer**: Broker pays you 25-50% of their commission
- **You sourced the seller**: Broker pays you 25-50% of their commission
- **You sourced both**: You keep full commission (or split 50-50 if broker does site visit)

- Target: 10 brokers = ₹20K-50K/month

- Like 99acres premium but WhatsApp-native

- Charge ₹2,000-5,000 to verify a deal through reputed brokers
- You verify: property documents, ownership, encumbrance
- Partner with established Chandigarh brokers for verification
- Build reputation as trusted deal authenticator

## Deal Authentication Network

### How to Build Trusted Broker Network

1. **Identify top 10-15 brokers** in each area (99acres, MagicBricks ratings)
2. **Offer them free leads** for first month — build relationship
3. **Authentication process**:
   - Seller provides property docs to broker
   - Broker verifies ownership, legal status
   - You mark listing as "Verified ✓" in your system
   - Verified listings get priority in matching
4. **Broker earns**: Fixed verification fee + priority on matched deals

### Recommended Broker Partners

Look for brokers with:
- 5+ years in Chandigarh Tricity
- RERA registration
- Good reviews on 99acres/MagicBricks
- Active in WhatsApp groups (you'll spot them!)
- Specialization in specific areas (Sector specialist, commercial specialist, etc.)

## Key Success Factors

1. **Volume of groups**: More groups = more listings = more matches
2. **Speed of response**: First to connect buyer-seller wins the deal
3. **Trust building**: Verified listings get 3x more responses
4. **Broker network**: Good brokers bring repeat business
5. **Follow-up**: 80% of deals need 3-5 follow-ups to close

## Tips for Chandigarh Tricity Market

- **Hot areas right now**: Zirakpur (affordable), New Chandigarh (growing), Mohali IT City
- **Peak season**: Oct-March (wedding season drives home buying)
- **Price range sweet spot**: ₹30L-1Cr flats/plots (maximum volume)
- **Weekend warriors**: Most site visits happen Saturday-Sunday
- **RERA check**: Always verify RERA registration for under-construction projects
- **SCO/SCF**: Commercial properties in Mohali have high demand — good commissions
